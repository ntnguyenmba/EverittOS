# EverittOS sell-ready operating checklist

EverittOS is positioned for cleaning, make-ready, maintenance, turnover, and small property-service operators who want the daily workflow in one place.

## Core sales story

**From request to paid job without the spreadsheet, text-message, and photo chaos.**

Show this workflow in demos:

1. Lead or public estimate request enters EverittOS.
2. Build an estimate or use Quotes to check costs, margin, similar jobs, and optional public market context.
3. Convert the approved work into a job.
4. Schedule and assign the worker.
5. Give the worker a clear job packet and checklist.
6. Capture before/after photos and completion details.
7. Invoice or collect payment.
8. Send a completion, review, payment, or recurring-service follow-up.
9. Use the dashboard to see revenue, work, customers, and outstanding items.

## Sell-ready features

- Leads and public forms
- Estimates
- Quotes with operator-controlled final pricing
- Job Assistant for rough notes, scope, worker checklist, supplies, instructions, and customer summary
- Customer follow-up message builder
- Customers
- Jobs and scheduling
- Worker/team workflows
- Photos
- Invoices and payments
- Expenses and bookkeeping
- English, Spanish, and Vietnamese product workflows
- Shared inactivity/session policy for web and installed apps

## Quote safety language

Pricing suggestions are decision support, not guaranteed market prices. The operator chooses the final price. Keep the product language clear that suggestions can use the operator's costs, past jobs, and optional public context.

## Demo script

Use one realistic job from beginning to end instead of showing every menu item. A strong demo is a cleaning or make-ready request with a messy customer text. Turn it into a job packet in Assistant, check the price in Quotes, show the job/worker experience, then show the customer follow-up and money flow.

## Metrics to watch

- Lead to estimate conversion
- Estimate or quote to job conversion
- Time from request to sendable price
- Jobs completed per workspace
- Weekly active workspaces
- Repeat customers
- Payments collected
- Outstanding balances
- 30, 60, and 90 day retention

## Product discipline

Do not add broad unrelated modules until customer usage shows a repeated need. Prioritize reliability and the request to quote to job to completion to payment loop.
